Questions for readers

Is DAI a general-purpose assistant?

No. The manuscript describes a domain-specific LLM and a defensive architecture, with model output excluded from execution authority.

Can I verify the reported architecture from a paper?

The papers specify the mechanisms and recomputation contract. Some properties require inspecting construction or provider capabilities; a receipt does not prove every property. DAI §8.10 explicitly discusses target-provenance limitations.

Are the papers peer-reviewed?

These editions are research preprints. Formal results depend on the stated assumptions; reported observations are specific to the experiments described. No third-party scientific endorsement is asserted.

May I use the proprietary software or model weights?

The public repository hosts research manuscripts, citations, a technical glossary and the research website. Public access does not grant a license to proprietary source code or model weights.
